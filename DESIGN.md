# PatchArena design system

PatchArena uses an editorial developer-tool aesthetic: warm paper, near-black terminal surfaces, signal orange for actions and acid lime only for verified output. Layouts follow a strict grid, cards use one-pixel rules rather than decorative shadows, and transactional states remain explicit.

- Ink `#101522`; paper `#f5f1e8`; orange `#ff6a2a`; acid `#d9ff72`.
- Display type is heavy and tightly tracked; technical labels use monospace uppercase.
- Motion is limited to 180–250ms interaction feedback and disabled under reduced motion.
- All controls require visible focus, minimum 44px targets and truthful empty/error states.
- Never use generic AI gradients, glassmorphism, fake bounties or unverified success messages.
